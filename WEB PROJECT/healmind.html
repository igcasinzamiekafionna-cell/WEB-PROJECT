HealMind

HealMind is a website to help people better understand their mental health and emotional well-being. Users can provide personal information and answer questions related to their current situation. The information will be treated as confidential and used to provide personalized guidance. The website will also provide recommendations and helpful resources based on the user's responses, including possible steps they can take to improve their mental well-being. Professional psychologist may also contribute information and guidance to ensure that the content is reliable and appropriate.

Technology Used:

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • PHP/Laravel
  • MySQL
  • Secure authentication and database system
