FameFeed

This is a social media-style website where users can introduce themselves and share parts of their everyday lives. Users can upload daily photos or short videos with a maximum length of five minutes and add captions written like personal diary entries. The platform will allow users to discover posts from other people through their feeds. Users can interact with content and potentially gain popularity as their posts are introduced to a wider audience.

Technology Used:

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • PHP/Laravel/Node.js
  • MySQL/MongoDB
  • Cloud Storage for Images and Videos
