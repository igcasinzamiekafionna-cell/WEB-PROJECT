Solace

Anti-Bullying website and digital platform designed to promote awareness and provide support for students experiencing bullying. The platform will allow students to report bullying incidents and quickly request help when they feel threatened or unsafe. A digital emergency button can be included so that a student can immediately send an alert to designated school staff or authorities when an incident occurs. The system can also maintain confidential records of reported incidents to help schools identify recurring problems and provide appropriate support. Rather than publicly displaying the names of students accused of bullying, the system should protect students' privacy and ensure that reports are reviewed by authorized school personnel before any action is taken.

Technology Used:

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • PHP/Laravel
  • MySQL
  • User Authentication
  • Notification/Alert System
  • GPS/Location Services
  • Mobile-Responsive Design
