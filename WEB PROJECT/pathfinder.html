PathFinder

Career Path is a website designed for students and individuals who are undecided about their future careers. The platform will introduce different career options and provide information about the skills, education, qualifications, and career paths required for each profession. Users will also be able to explore possible salary ranges and learn about companies where they may apply for jobs or internships. The goal is to help students make informed decisions about their future careers.

Technology Used:

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • PHP/Laravel
  • MySQL
  • API integration for job and salary
